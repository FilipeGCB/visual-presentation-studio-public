# QA Severity

## BLOCKER

Wrong fact or number, serious contradiction, invented information, broken primary navigation, unreadable essential content, or a broken presentation surface. A BLOCKER denies delivery and requires correction plus revalidation.

## MAJOR

A serious hierarchy, narrative, density, chart-comprehension, interaction-complexity, or approved-direction divergence problem. A MAJOR materially impairs comprehension, trust, or use and denies `READY FOR USER REVIEW`.

## MINOR

Polish that does not materially impair comprehension or use, such as localized spacing, microcopy, fine contrast, timing, or alignment. Record known MINOR issues and decide whether they are worth correcting before Gate 2.

## Issue lifecycle

`open → fixed → revalidated`

An issue is not closed merely because source code changed. Revalidate against the rendered output or the relevant technical evidence.
