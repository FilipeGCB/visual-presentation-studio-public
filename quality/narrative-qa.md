# Narrative QA

Record PASS, FAIL, or NOT APPLICABLE with evidence.

- The thesis can be stated in one specific sentence.
- The opening frames the question, stakes, or desired understanding.
- Each screen or block passes: “This unit exists to ______.”
- Each screen or block passes the removal test or is removed.
- Evidence is positioned with the claim it supports.
- Transitions do not depend on hidden premises.
- Repetition adds emphasis, contrast, or synthesis rather than padding.
- Exploratory branches preserve orientation and return to the thesis.
- The conclusion resolves the opening and identifies the intended decision, action, or understanding.
- The title of every major unit expresses a claim or meaningful role rather than a generic label.

A confusing sequence that materially weakens understanding is MAJOR.
