# Generic AI UI

## Signal

The result combines excessive rounded cards, purposeless glassmorphism, generic gradients, repeated three-card grids, decorative neon, generic icons, dashboard-style metric tiles, and motion on every element. The visual could accept unrelated content without changing its structure.

## Why it fails

It lets familiar components choose the narrative, erases hierarchy, and creates an interchangeable “AI premium” look. Novelty becomes decoration rather than clarity.

## Detection

- Can the content be swapped for another topic with almost no compositional change?
- Are cards used because items truly share a comparison model?
- Does each gradient, blur, glow, icon, and animation have a stated function?
- Has quantitative content been turned into a dashboard without an exploratory need?
- Are titles claims, or generic labels such as “Overview” and “Key Insights”?

## Correction

Return to the thesis and storyboard. Establish one primary hierarchy, choose components per narrative function, replace generic labels with claims, remove decorative effects, and use assets only where they prove, explain, orient, compare, humanize, or create intentional rhythm.
