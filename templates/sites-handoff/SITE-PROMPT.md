# Publication Execution Prompt

Build and publish the experience described by the accompanying handoff package.

## Preserve exactly

- objective and audience;
- thesis and primary narrative;
- approved visual direction;
- structure and hierarchy;
- asset functions and approved sources;
- primary interactions and state behavior;
- restrictions and frozen Gate 1 decisions;
- quality and accessibility criteria.

Implementation details may be translated to the publication environment when necessary. Do not reinterpret approved creative decisions, replace the narrative with a generic site pattern, or alter frozen choices without returning for explicit approval.

## Required behavior

1. Read `SITE-BRIEF.md`, `design-spec.md`, `interaction-spec.md`, content, and asset records before implementation.
2. Use the smallest stack supported by the approved experience.
3. Preserve local-first assets and avoid adding remote dependencies without a documented need.
4. Render and inspect the real published result at the required viewports.
5. Correct every known BLOCKER and MAJOR before presenting the URL for Gate 2.
6. Return the publication URL, build or deployment evidence, QA record, and any remaining MINOR issue.
