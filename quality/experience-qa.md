# Experience QA

Record PASS, FAIL, or NOT APPLICABLE with the state exercised.

- The initial state explains where the audience is and what can be done.
- Primary navigation works with expected pointer, keyboard, and touch input.
- Focus order is logical and visible focus is never suppressed.
- Filters, tabs, toggles, comparisons, selections, and drill-down states communicate selection and effect.
- Return and reset paths restore an understandable state.
- Empty, unavailable, loading, and error states are clear where relevant.
- Controls use semantic elements and accessible names.
- Motion directs attention or clarifies change; `prefers-reduced-motion` preserves meaning without nonessential transition.
- Scroll-linked zoom, crop, parallax, sticky media, and state swaps have a named narrative function and preserve native scrolling.
- Every scrollytelling chapter synchronizes copy, media, active status, and progress in forward and reverse movement.
- Progress and location feedback match the actual state.
- Interactions increase comprehension, exploration, or narrative rhythm.
- Mobile and narrow viewports remain usable without precision tapping or hidden controls.
- A static mobile crop is recorded as partial evidence until the live mobile route and touch behavior are exercised.

A broken primary path is BLOCKER. An interaction that makes the experience materially harder to understand is MAJOR.
