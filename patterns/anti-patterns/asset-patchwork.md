# Asset Patchwork

## Signal

Images are generated or selected after layout and share no consistent camera, lighting, material, palette, crop logic, text-safe area, or narrative progression. Direction approval is described as approval of photographs that the user never reviewed.

## Why it fails

Individually polished images do not create a visual system. Unrelated assets break continuity, weaken identity, and make premium composition feel patched together. Ambiguous approval language also destroys the decision trail.

## Detection

- Was each primary asset briefed in the storyboard before generation or selection?
- Do the assets form one commissioned family or two deliberately separated editorial registers?
- Is there a mother scene or another explicit series logic when states evolve through scroll?
- Are focal point, text-safe area, desktop/mobile crops, and narrative state recorded?
- Can the approval ledger identify the exact asset the user approved?
- Was the asset validated inside the rendered composition and intended interaction state?

## Correction

Return to the reference matrix, storyboard, and Asset Board Checkpoint. Define the series language, create or select named candidates, obtain the scoped Gate 1 asset decision, integrate only approved-for-production assets, and validate every primary asset in the settled rendered composition.

## Do not overapply

Minor icons, utility marks, and evidence screenshots do not require an asset-board ceremony unless they materially determine identity or composition.
