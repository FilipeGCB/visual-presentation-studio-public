# Content QA

Record PASS, FAIL, or NOT APPLICABLE with evidence for each relevant check.

- Thesis and key claims match the approved Gate 1 contract.
- Names, dates, labels, units, currencies, totals, percentages, denominators, and time ranges are correct.
- Calculations and derived values were independently checked.
- Chart values, labels, annotations, text, and interaction states are consistent.
- External or current claims have traceable sources appropriate to the use.
- Supplied facts, verified research, assumptions, and fictional examples are clearly distinguished.
- No fact, quote, data point, finding, or conclusion was invented.
- Confidential or personal content appears only where authorized.
- Editing for brevity did not remove a condition, caveat, or essential meaning.
- Language, tone, and level of detail fit the audience.

Any wrong or invented material is a BLOCKER.
